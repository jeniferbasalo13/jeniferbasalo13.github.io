How I work

The same five steps every working day, whether the paperwork is a lumber invoice, a leaking faucet or a new lead.

  1. JE-01

    Capture

    Pull in everything new: bills, vendor emails, tenant requests, leads. Nothing lives only in an inbox.

  2. JE-02

    Code

    Every bill gets a vendor, GL account and property or job. Every repair gets a work-order number.

  3. JE-03

    Match

    Invoice against PO or approved work order. W-9 and EFT on file before a payment is queued.

  4. JE-04

    Reconcile

    Tie the books to bank and statement balances. Find the difference; never plug it.

  5. JE-05

    Report

    A short update for the owner: what's paid, what needs approval, and the sales and lead numbers.
